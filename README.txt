Field Drafting Sheet - iPhone web app

Upload ALL of these files, side by side, to the top level of your website (for example a GitHub Pages repository):
  index.html
  manifest.webmanifest
  sw.js
  apple-touch-icon.png
  icon-192.png
  icon-512.png
  icon-maskable-512.png

Then open the site in Safari on your iPhone, tap the three-dot (or Share) button, choose
Share > Add to Home Screen, keep "Open as Web App" on, and tap Add.

Updating later: replace index.html, change VERSION in sw.js (e.g. fds-1 to fds-2), upload both,
then open the app on your phone twice (the first launch downloads the update).

Your drawings are stored on each phone, not on the website. Use "Save / load data" > "Save backup file" regularly.
